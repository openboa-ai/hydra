## OpenBoa handoff

- Assignment and Issue: [purpose, outcome, and link]
- Current Work lead: [human or agent role]
- Next Work lead: [human or agent role]
- Decision rights: [authority that continues, changes, or expires]
- Resources: [repositories, context, tools, environments, access, time, and budget used or remaining]
- Boundaries: [conditions that still require escalation]
- Current state and plan: [concise summary]
- Material decisions and commitments: [links or summary]
- Changed artifacts: [files, commits, pull requests, or other links]
- Evidence: [checks, reviews, previews, delivery, or observation]
- Remaining work: [ordered outcomes, not keystrokes]
- Blocker or decision: [exact need, owner, and consequence—or none]
- Next safe action: [one action the next lead can take]
- Expiry or revisit time: [date/time or not applicable]

OpenBoa accountability is inherited and is not repeated in handoffs. Record a human only when specific delegated decision authority must transfer or expire. The next lead confirms or challenges the recovered assignment before continuing. Do not declare completion until acceptance, required delivery, and observation evidence exist.
