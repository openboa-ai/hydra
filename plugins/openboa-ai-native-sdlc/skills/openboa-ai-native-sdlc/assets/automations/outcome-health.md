# Outcome health

- Target: one durable Issue and its linked delivery surfaces.
- Cadence: no faster than the product signal can change; declare timezone and expiry.
- Read: Issue state, linked pull requests, deployment revision, acceptance signals, regressions, and missing evidence.
- Evaluate: distinguish implemented, verified, delivered, observed, and measured.
- Notify: outcome drift, stale evidence, regression, or a named unblock decision.
- Stop: observation window completes with qualified evidence, the outcome is superseded, or rollback begins.
