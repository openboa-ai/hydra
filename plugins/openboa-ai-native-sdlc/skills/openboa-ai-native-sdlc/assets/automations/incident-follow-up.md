# Incident follow-up

- Target: one incident, its impacted environment, corrective work, and observation window.
- Wake: corrective commit, deployment, rollback, alert transition, or review date.
- Reconcile before retrying any mitigation; determine whether an external effect already occurred.
- Track: user impact, current safety, causal evidence, corrective action, owner of the next decision, recurrence test, and observation.
- Notify: impact worsens, recovery stalls, evidence contradicts the current cause, or a consequential action is required.
- Stop: recovery and recurrence prevention are observed, or a human accepts a time-bounded residual risk.
