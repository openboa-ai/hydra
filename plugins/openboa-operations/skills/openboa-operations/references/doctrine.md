# OpenBoa Doctrine

**Status:** DRAFT — non-normative until explicit approval by `SonSangjoon`
**Contract:** `0.1.0`
**Language:** English

## Vision

OpenBoa expands the horizon of human possibility by reducing the distance between understanding, imagination, and observable realization.

## Era thesis

Software is moving from a system in which people must prescribe every procedure to a system in which agents can receive goals, inspect environments, choose actions, and iterate toward outcomes. Execution becomes more abundant, parallel, and portable. The scarce capabilities become clear purpose, useful context, judgment, trust, accountability, and the ability to choose which possible futures deserve realization.

## Human and agent

An agent is a delegated actor. It may investigate, imagine alternatives, plan, create, test, and operate with substantial autonomy inside an explicit authority corridor. Delegation does not transfer ownership of purpose, public commitments, irreversible decisions, or organizational accountability. A named human owns every durable goal even when one or more agents perform the work.

## Principles

### Possibility over inherited precedent

Look for what the present conditions make possible before treating inherited convention as a boundary. Question precedent when it hides a better outcome, but do not confuse imagination with evidence.

### Realization gives imagination meaning

An idea becomes operationally meaningful when it can be turned into a testable, observable outcome. Move from concept to a small reversible realization and learn from the environment.

### Goals over sessions and artifacts

The durable goal is the unit of work. A Codex task, worktree, run attempt, branch, PR, or document is a replaceable means of advancing it.

### Evidence over assertion

Prefer executable checks, environment state, reviewable diffs, deployment records, and observation data over claims that work is complete.

### Context is infrastructure

Repository knowledge, local commands, acceptance criteria, decision records, and handoff artifacts are part of the operating system. Keep them small, current, discoverable, and versioned.

### Autonomy inside explicit authority

Grant the widest autonomy that the goal, risk lane, workspace boundary, and policy allow. Tighten authority at the boundary of secrets, identity, security, privacy, money, irreversible state, and public commitments.

### One accountable owner, many contributors

Agents may be delegated contributors and may create follow-up work. A human owner remains accountable for the goal, the decision, and the outcome.

### Completion is an observed outcome

Completion is not a final message or a green process exit. It requires the acceptance criteria, delivery state, and required post-delivery observation to be satisfied with durable evidence.

### Failures improve the system

When an agent fails, improve the smallest useful layer: context, tool, test, evaluator, guardrail, or workflow. Do not rely on increasingly long prompts or repeated manual nudges as the default fix.

### Portable meaning, local implementation

The meaning of a goal, state, owner, evidence, and gate should survive a change of agent or work environment. Codex plugin manifests, GitHub workflows, and repository commands are adapters, not the doctrine itself.
