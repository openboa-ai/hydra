# Doctrine

This page is a navigation surface. The canonical doctrine is [OpenBoa AI-Native SDLC Doctrine](plugins/openboa-ai-native-sdlc/skills/openboa-ai-native-sdlc/references/doctrine.md).

It explains the stable foundation of the system:

- OpenBoa's purpose and its view of agents as a new dimension of creation;
- the relationship between human purpose, agent-led work, and system boundaries;
- what makes a development lifecycle AI-native; and
- which principles should survive changes in methods, models, and platforms.

For how those principles become responsibilities, continue to the [Operating Model](OPERATING-MODEL.md). For the evidence behind the design, see the [research basis](plugins/openboa-ai-native-sdlc/skills/openboa-ai-native-sdlc/references/research-basis.md).
