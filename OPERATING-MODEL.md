# Operating Model

This page is a navigation surface. The canonical operating model is [OpenBoa AI-Native SDLC Operating Model](plugins/openboa-ai-native-sdlc/skills/openboa-ai-native-sdlc/references/operating-model.md).

It defines the current organizational interfaces:

- human, agent work lead, contributor, evaluator, and system responsibilities;
- agent-led, joint, and human-led decision boundaries;
- durable work units, states, and continuity across sessions and models; and
- the responsibilities of Hydra, product repositories, Codex, and GitHub.

For execution, follow the [lifecycle](plugins/openboa-ai-native-sdlc/skills/openboa-ai-native-sdlc/references/lifecycle.md), [authority and approvals](plugins/openboa-ai-native-sdlc/skills/openboa-ai-native-sdlc/references/authority-and-approvals.md), and [Codex and GitHub](plugins/openboa-ai-native-sdlc/skills/openboa-ai-native-sdlc/references/codex-and-github.md) references.
