# Governance

This page is a route into the canonical OpenBoa AI-Native SDLC guidance. It does not define a separate policy.

- [Authority and approvals](plugins/openboa-ai-native-sdlc/skills/openboa-ai-native-sdlc/references/authority-and-approvals.md) explains agent-led work, exact human gates, permissions, and exceptions.
- [Codex and GitHub](plugins/openboa-ai-native-sdlc/skills/openboa-ai-native-sdlc/references/codex-and-github.md) explains the execution and control-plane split, Issues, pull requests, rulesets, Actions, and continuity.
- [Evaluation and learning](plugins/openboa-ai-native-sdlc/skills/openboa-ai-native-sdlc/references/evaluation-and-learning.md) explains completion evidence, metrics, observation, and durable improvement.
- [Capability map](plugins/openboa-ai-native-sdlc/skills/openboa-ai-native-sdlc/references/capability-map.md) separates available execution mechanisms from authority.
- [Non-goals and v0.2 boundaries](plugins/openboa-ai-native-sdlc/skills/openboa-ai-native-sdlc/references/non-goals.md) states what this foundation does not attempt to build or authorize.

Start with [DOCTRINE.md](DOCTRINE.md) for the stable purpose, [OPERATING-MODEL.md](OPERATING-MODEL.md) for responsibilities and decisions, and [AI-NATIVE-SDLC.md](AI-NATIVE-SDLC.md) for the lifecycle and playbooks.
